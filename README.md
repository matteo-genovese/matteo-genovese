I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from **Palermo, Italy**, currently based in Rome, pursuing a Master's in **Computer Science & AI** at **Sapienza**.

I work in **mobile network operations at Sparkle** - SS7, Diameter, GTP, roaming, multi-IMSI - on systems where reliability is measured rather than assumed.

I also lead **infrastructure and backend at [Avascot](https://avascot.com)**, a creative tech studio, and I
co-founded **[NoveZero](https://novezero.it)**, a web agency in Palermo.

> 🎯 Growing toward **Cloud / Infrastructure Architect** and **Platform Engineer** roles: architecture I can
> defend, infrastructure I have actually broken and repaired.
